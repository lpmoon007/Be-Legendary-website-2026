## Why this exists

The Otterly GEO audit (Oct 2026) found belegendary's **on-site content and technical GEO are already excellent** — answer-first pages, FAQ/HowTo/Article schema, the Flag Model named as the answer, every AI crawler explicitly allowed in robots.txt, clean llms.txt + sitemap. Yet belegendary is cited on only **7 of 14** executive-team prompts, is **absent** from the core "how/why" questions it should own, and shows up on **only 3 of 7** AI engines (ChatGPT, Copilot, Google — nothing on Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Claude).

**The binding constraint is not content. It's off-page authority.** AI engines assemble answers from sources they *retrieve and trust* — overwhelmingly (1) established high-authority domains (HBR, McKinsey, CCL, Korn Ferry) and (2) third-party "best-of" roundup lists. belegendary.org is a newer domain that isn't on those lists and hasn't earned that trust yet. A perfect page on a low-authority domain still loses to a mediocre HBR page. This playbook earns the authority.

**The three levers, in priority order:** A) roundup inclusion (fastest) · B) backlinks & earned mentions · C) entity graph.

**Baseline measured ✅ (2026-10-22):** belegendary.org = **AS 12 / 326 referring domains**, vs CCL (57 / 17.2K), Korn Ferry (49 / 27.8K), Vistage (44 / 12.7K) — a 32–45-pt authority gap and ~40–85× fewer referring domains. Full snapshot + link-gap targets in **`GEO-SEMRUSH-BASELINE.md`**. Re-pull in ~90 days. The numbers confirm the thesis: the gap is off-page authority, not content.

## Track C — Entity graph *(makes LLMs treat you as a known entity)*

AI models resolve and trust *entities*. The more corroborated "Be Legendary = executive-team performance firm; James Carter = founder & Flag Model creator" is across the web's structured knowledge, the more confidently models cite you.

**C1 — Wikidata (items exist — strengthen them).** Be Legendary = **Q140513581**, James Carter = **Q140514540** (already in your schema `sameAs`). Add/verify statements: instance-of (consulting/advisory firm), founder, founder's notable works (the books), official website, industry, located-in, described-at-URL, with references. **Create a Wikidata item for "The Flag Model"** (the concept) — creator: James Carter; described-at belegendary.org/flag-model/ — so the framework is its own resolvable entity. *Claude can draft the exact statement set.*

**C2 — Wikipedia (assess, then pursue carefully).** James Carter may clear notability (CNN, CNN Money, Business Insider; books co-authored with Covey/Blanchard/Chopra/Tracy). Be Legendary likely needs more independent coverage first. **Caution:** Wikipedia enforces strict notability + conflict-of-interest rules — a self-written article is deleted. The path is independent reliable-source coverage (which Track B produces) → a neutral editor creates it. Treat this as an *outcome of B*, not a first move.

**C3 — Consistency.** Schema/sameAs/NAP are already strong (Wikidata, LinkedIn, Amazon author, Crunchbase, Building Teams). Keep them identical on every new profile.
