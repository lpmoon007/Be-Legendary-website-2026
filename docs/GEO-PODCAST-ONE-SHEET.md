## The one-sheet *(paste into an email or a PDF)*

### James Carter
**Founder, Be Legendary · Creator of the Flag Model™**
*Where does your executive team break first?*

Twenty-five years inside hundreds of executive teams. Author of the forthcoming *Lost Disciplines of Leadership™*; collaborative author alongside Deepak Chopra and Ken Blanchard (*Roadmap to Success*) and Brian Tracy, Ken Blanchard and Stephen Covey (*Discover Your Inner Strength*). Featured in **CNN, CNN Money, and Business Insider.** Keynote speaker to rooms of 1,000+ leaders.

**The hook James brings to a show:** most leadership teams don't fail because they don't know what to do — they fail because *one* discipline quietly broke, and nobody measured it. James can name which one, on air, for a host's own team or a listener's.

### Why he's a great guest
- **One clean, contrarian thesis** that reframes a problem every leader has: *the strategy isn't the problem; a broken discipline is.*
- **Tells stories with numbers** — real engagements with measured turnarounds, not platitudes.
- **Gives the audience something to do** — a free self-assessment listeners can take mid-episode (belegendary.org).
- **Media-trained and remote-ready** — studio-quality audio/video, books around to reference, flexible on length.

### Episode angles (pick one, or let the host choose)
1. **"Why your team knows what to do and still doesn't do it"** — the feedback vacuum, and the Flag Model fix.
2. **"Trust is the output, not the input"** — the counterintuitive order to rebuild a dysfunctional team.
3. **"Your leadership offsite didn't stick — here's the weekly habit that would have."**
4. **"Measuring execution"** — what a FlagScore™ is, and how you put a number on whether a team actually follows through.
5. **"Candor can make it worse"** — when more honesty damages a team, and what to do instead (ties to the research notes).

### Signature stories (host can ask for the numbers)
- A team that went from stalled to a **268% lift** after one discipline was named and rebuilt.
- **Champion Iron** — a **209% measured lift** in the metric that mattered.
- The **"$1.2B against seven rival firms"** turnaround — how a single broken discipline was costing a company the market.
*(James has the full context for each — these are illustrative, confirm specifics with him before a host cites a figure on air.)*

### Sample questions for the host
- What's the first thing that breaks on an executive team — and how do you spot it before it's a crisis?
- You say "truth over comfort." What does that cost a leader in the room?
- If a CEO has 30 days and one move, what is it?
- What's a "feedback vacuum," and why is it more dangerous than open conflict?

### Soundbites *(for show-note pull quotes)*
> "Teams don't drown in the big storms. They drown in the one leak nobody named."
> "Trust isn't what you start with. It's what you get when the disciplines hold."
> "You can't fix what you won't measure — and most teams never put a number on execution."

## Target-show list

Priority = fit (executive-team / leadership audience) × likelihood of a site link in show notes. **Find current contact + booking link before sending** (most have a "be a guest" form or a booker email on the site).

### Tier 1 — leadership/CEO shows that book author-practitioners *(best fit)*
| Show | Why it fits | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Coaching for Leaders** (Dave Stachowiak) | long-running, books framework authors, strong show notes w/ links | Angle 1 or 2 |
| **The Look & Sound of Leadership** / **Dose of Leadership** | exec-coaching audience, practitioner guests | Angle 3 |
| **The Leadership Podcast** (Jan Rutherford & Jim Vaselopulos) | senior-leader audience, loves a framework | Angle 1 or 4 |
| **At The Table w/ Patrick Lencioni** *(aspirational)* | *Five Dysfunctions* audience = exact ICP; hard to get but perfect | Angle 2 (position Flag Model as complementary, never vs. Lencioni) |
| **Craig Groeschel Leadership Podcast** *(aspirational)* | huge leadership audience | Angle 1 |

### Tier 2 — business/management shows with execution/teams themes
| Show | Angle |
|---|---|
| **HBR IdeaCast** *(aspirational — pitch the research report, not a pitch)* | Angle 4, led with the data |
| **The Modern Manager** (Mamie Kanfer Stewart) | Angle 3 — tactical, practical |
| **Negotiate Anything / Nobody's Listening-type candor shows** | Angle 5 (candor) |
| **Dear HBR / management-advice formats** | Angle 1 |

### Tier 3 — niche & easier yeses (build the cluster fast)
Industry-vertical leadership shows, EOS/Traction-adjacent operator podcasts, regional CEO roundtables, and solo-host leadership shows actively seeking guests (search "[leadership podcast] + 'guest'" / PodMatch / MatchMaker.fm). Lower individual authority, but they say yes, they link, and they compound. **A VA can book several of these per week.**

### Skip / handle with care
Don't pitch James *against* Lencioni, EOS, or OKRs on their own creators' shows — position the Flag Model as the *diagnostic* layer (find the break), complementary to whatever operating system they run. The "vs" framing belongs on our own comparison pages, not on a host's turf.
