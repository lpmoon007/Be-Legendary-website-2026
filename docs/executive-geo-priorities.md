`corporate_meeting_team_building`, `executive_team_building`,
`executive_team_development`, `executive_retreats` — filtered to the
executive / senior-leader / board buyer only.

**Goal:** more mentions and citations in AI answers (GEO), plus stronger
conversion, on belegendary.org. Intent across every executive cluster is
overwhelmingly *informational* (60–80%), so we win by being the best-structured,
most-quotable **answer** — answer-first copy, a clean definition block, at least
one attributable stat, a named primitive (Flag Model / FlagScore / feedback
vacuum), and FAQPage/Article schema on every executive page.

Work these **one at a time, top to bottom.** Update Status as we go so nothing
is lost across sessions.

## Explicitly out of scope (noise — NOT the buyer)
Do **not** build for these, even though they appear in the data:
executive search / recruiting / staffing; job & career moves; MBA / executive
education degrees; spiritual / wellness / rehab / religious / family / youth
retreats; MS Teams / meeting software; icebreakers / virtual games; city- and
venue-specific team-building.

## Recommendation
Start at **P1** — largest, fastest-growing, highest-commercial executive pool,
and we already own the contrarian position; it just isn't built to the scale the
demand warrants.
