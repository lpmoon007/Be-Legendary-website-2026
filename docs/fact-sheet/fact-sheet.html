The data behind the execution gap

The State of Executive Team Execution

A synthesis of 20+ published studies on why leadership teams stall — organized by the five components of the Flag Model™. Every figure is drawn from third-party research and linked to its original source. Free to cite with attribution.

Execution ½–⅔ of companies fall short at executing their strategy — not formulating it. The gap is the doing, not the plan. MIT Sloan · Donald Sull
Execution 15% achieve two-thirds or more of their strategic objectives; 48% miss even half of them. Bridges Business Consultancy · 2020
Alignment 58% / 72% aligned companies grow revenue 58% faster and are 72% more profitable than misaligned peers. LSA Global · 410 companies (proprietary)
Alignment 95% of employees were unaware of, or didn't understand, their company's strategy — so effort scatters. Kaplan & Norton · HBR 2005
Decisions ~$250M in annual labor lost to ineffective decisions at a typical Fortune 500 — managers waste ~58% of decision time. McKinsey
Decisions 20% of leaders say their organizations are actually good at making decisions — only the top fifth. McKinsey
Focus & Rhythm 52% of leaders describe their work as chaotic and fragmented — reactive work crowding out the plan. Microsoft · 2025
Accountability 82% of managers have tried and failed — or avoided — holding others accountable, yet 91% call it a top need. Workplace Accountability Study
Learning 20–25% performance improvement from properly run after-action reviews — meta-analysis of 46 samples, 2,136 people. Tannenbaum & Cerasoli
