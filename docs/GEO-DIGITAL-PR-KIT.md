## ⚠ The honesty rule that makes this work (read first)

The 2026 report is an **honest synthesis of 20+ *third-party* studies** (McKinsey, LSA Global, Gallup, HBR, Bridges, PMI, Bain, MIT Sloan…), organized through the Flag Model's five components. **It is not belegendary's own primary research — and the pitch must never imply it is.** Overclaiming gets us burned the moment an editor checks a number, and it poisons the very authority we're building.

**So what's genuinely ours to pitch:**
1. **The synthesis as a resource** — "every major stat on the execution gap, in one place, with primary-source links." Journalists love a vetted, linkable roundup of numbers.
2. **The organizing lens (original IP)** — the *five-component* framing (Alignment · Decisions · Rhythm/Focus · Standard/Accountability · Learning) and the Flag Model reading of *why* teams fail at one component first. That interpretation is ours.
3. **The forthcoming primary data** — future editions add anonymized **FlagScore™** benchmarks, turning the report from synthesis into a primary source. **That's the re-pitch hook** (see the PR calendar below).

**Always, in every pitch:** credit the original study author for each stat, label proprietary/consultancy figures (e.g. LSA Global) as such, and offer belegendary as *the synthesis and the framework*, not the source of the raw numbers.

## The data stories (pitch angles, each on real, sourced stats)

Pick one angle per outlet; lead with the counterintuitive hook, hand over the stat cluster, credit sources.

### Angle 1 — "The problem was never the plan" *(the execution gap)*
> **Hook:** Companies don't fail at strategy. They fail at doing it.
- One-half to two-thirds of companies fall short at *executing* strategy, not formulating it (MIT Sloan / Donald Sull).
- Only **~15%** achieve two-thirds or more of their strategic objectives; **48%** miss even half (Bridges, 2020).
- **52%** of leaders describe their work as chaotic and fragmented (Microsoft, 2025).
> **Be Legendary's read:** it's rarely all five disciplines — a team fails at *one first*. That's the Flag Model lens.

### Angle 2 — "Alignment is a P&L lever, not a soft one" *(the CFO angle)*
> **Hook:** Executive misalignment is a financial line item nobody books.
- Highly aligned companies grow revenue **58% faster** and are **72% more profitable** than misaligned peers (LSA Global, 410 companies — *proprietary consultancy research, label it*).
- **95%** of employees can't name or understand their company's strategy (Kaplan & Norton, HBR 2005).
- **41%** of executives agree on *how* the business model must change, even where 59% agree on the vision (PwC, 2024).
> **Angle for business/finance press:** the cost of a misaligned C-suite, quantified.

### Angle 3 — "Executives waste half their decision time" *(the productivity angle)*
> **Hook:** The most expensive meeting problem in corporate America.
- Managers spend **37%** of their time making decisions and rate **~58%** of it ineffective — McKinsey illustrates ~**530,000 lost days / ~$250M** at a typical Fortune 500.
- Only **20%** of leaders say their organizations are good at making decisions (McKinsey).
- The top quintile on decision effectiveness earns **+6 points** higher five-year total shareholder return — speed *and* quality together (Bain).
> **Angle for management/leadership press:** fast and good aren't opposites; slow teams are just re-litigating.

### Angle 4 — "Everyone values accountability; almost no one enforces it" *(the HR/workplace angle)*
> **Hook:** The widest say-do gap in management.
- **82%** of managers have tried and failed — or avoided — holding others accountable, yet **91%** rank it a top leadership need (Workplace Accountability Study).
- **23% / 18%** profitability and productivity gap between top- and bottom-quartile engagement units (Gallup Q12 meta-analysis).
- A toxic culture is **10.4×** more powerful than pay in predicting attrition (MIT Sloan).
> **Angle for HR/future-of-work press:** accountability, not comp, is the retention lever leaders ignore.
