# GEO Target List — where the $75K+ leads actually get decided

**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Source:** Otterly citation exports (services 4,988 rows; retreats 9,445 rows), Oct 2026,
plus Semrush US search volumes. "Cites" = sum of times an AI engine cited a domain for
that prompt across ChatGPT / Perplexity / Copilot / Google AI / Gemini.

## How to read this

Two different GEO games, and we play them differently:

- **"Best / who / companies" prompts** are won by **third-party roundups**, not by your
  own pages. The move is to *get Be Legendary listed on the pages the AIs already pull
  from.* Outreach/PR, not content.
- **"How / why / what to do" problem prompts** are won by **the citeable answer page.**
  The move is to *own more of these with content*, which is the one area you already win.

Headline: **Be Legendary already has reach (cited in 9 of 14 money-prompts) but almost no
depth.** Except for two prompts you dominate, you're a single citation while competitors
stack 20–70. The job is depth on the right prompts, and getting onto the roundups.

## C. Content to build / fix (own-the-answer prompts)

Priority order — these are where you can win with a page, not outreach:

1. **Defend "why isn't my leadership team executing" / "what to do when..."** — you're #1.
   Keep these pages fresh and tightly answer-shaped; they're proof the model works.
2. **"executive team assessment and diagnostic tools"** (absent) — you sell the Executive
   Team Diagnostic, FlagScore, and LFS. Build the citeable asset that names them as the
   answer. Biggest product-aligned gap.
3. **"leadership simulation for executive teams"** (pos 2) — push to #1. Your
   `/what-is-a-leadership-simulation/` page exists; make it the definitive cited answer vs
   the sim-software vendors (forio/celemi/execsim), who sell software, not a facilitated
   executive read.
4. **"how to fix a dysfunctional leadership team"** (pos 6, have a page) and **"how to build
   a high performing executive team" / "decision making" / "aligned on priorities"**
   (absent, McKinsey/HBR own them) — restructure the existing how-to pages into clean,
   answer-first content that an AI will lift. You have the pages; they aren't being cited.

## D. What this says about the site positioning

- The valuable buyers ask AI "who are the best executive team coaching firms / development
  companies / alignment consultants" — and you're cited but shallow. **Depth + roundups**
  is the near-term lead play, not retreats.
- Retreats GEO is a roundup game you're mostly absent from; worth pursuing via listings and
  your own roundup, but it is lower-value and thinner-intent than the coaching cluster.
- So the site should be positioned around **executive-team-performance (buyable)**, with the
  content/GEO investment aimed at Section C, and an outreach effort aimed at Section B.
