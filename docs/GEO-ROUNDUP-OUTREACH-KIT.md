## Target list

Priority = how likely the target is to (a) be cited by AI engines and (b) actually accept an addition. **Owner:** James or a VA sends; Claude drafts any custom version on request.

### Tier 1 — editorial listicles that update on request *(best ROI)*
| Target | The list to get onto | How to approach |
|---|---|---|
| **peoplemanagingpeople.com** | "Best Leadership Development Companies / Programs" | Email the editor (byline on the post) or their contact form. Use Template A. They actively update. |
| **DesignRush** | "Top Leadership Development Companies" / "Top Management Consulting" | Create a free agency/company profile (self-serve) → then request listicle inclusion. |
| **Clutch** / **The Manifest** | "Top Leadership Training Companies" / "Leadership Development" | Claim a free profile; gather 2–3 client reviews (even informal) — reviews drive ranking + citations. |
| Independent "Best executive coaching / team development firms 2026" blog posts | the specific post | Find via a search for the exact phrase; email the author with Template A. Many are solo bloggers who update for a good addition. |

### Tier 2 — competitor / practitioner blogs that run roundups
*(They won't add a competitor to a "best" list, but they take expert contributions and quotes, which still earn the link/mention.)*
| Target | Angle |
|---|---|
| flashpointleadership.com · elevateleadership.com · lakgroup.com · gogradia.com | Guest article or expert quote (Template C), not list inclusion |
| boon-health.com · arielgroup.com | Contributed article / partnership angle |
| umbrex.com | Independent-consultant network — see if James qualifies to be listed as a member/expert |
| marklefko.com | "Leadership offsite facilitators" — lower priority post-retreat-split; James-as-facilitator still fits |

### Tier 3 — directories & profiles (self-serve, do once)
G2 · Clutch · DesignRush · Crunchbase (verify existing) · LinkedIn company page (optimize) · Google Business Profile. These feed both roundups and entity recognition (Track C).

### Skip
**Vistage, Korn Ferry, CCL, McKinsey** — these are competitors who publish their own roundups; they won't list you. Beat them by getting onto the *neutral* lists above and by owning your own (below).
