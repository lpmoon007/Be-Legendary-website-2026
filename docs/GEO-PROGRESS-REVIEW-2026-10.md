# GEO Progress Review — October 2026

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Inputs:** Otterly Be Legendary Brand Report (26 Oct, 22 prompts, US, all engines), the AI-performance overview (daily citations 7/26→10/24), and the full citation export (13,888 citations across the 22 prompts).
**Bottom line:** The strategy is working — and the data now tells us *exactly* where to push next. We've crossed from "absent" to **a top-5 cited source**, but not yet to **a named, recommended provider.** Closing that one gap is the whole game from here.

---

## The headline, in one paragraph

Three months ago belegendary was cited on 7 of 14 prompts and absent from most engines. Today **belegendary.org is a top-5 cited domain (11% domain coverage) — tied with hbr.org and *ahead of* mckinsey.com, vistage.com and kornferry.com (all 8%).** Daily citations are up ~3–4× since late July. Yet **brand coverage is still 1%** and we're not in the top-10 brand ranking. Translation: **AI now trusts our content enough to cite it, but doesn't yet treat "Be Legendary" as a firm to recommend.** That's the gap between content success and brand authority — and it's exactly what the off-page plays (roundups, digital PR, entity graph) close.

## The three numbers that look contradictory but aren't

| Metric | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain coverage** | **11%** (top 5, tied w/ HBR) | our *pages* show up as sources in 11% of answers — we've arrived as a source |
| **Citation volume** | 1.5% of all citations; **+765% trend ("New")** | still thin per answer, but growing explosively off a small base |
| **Brand coverage** | **1%** | the AI rarely *names* "Be Legendary" in its answer text — we're a source, not yet a recommendation |

And when we *do* appear, we're elite: **position 1.6** when named, **sentiment +61**, **likelihood-to-buy 87%** (tied with the category leaders, above Vistage's 66% and DDI's 78%). **Quality is not the problem. Volume and provider-recognition are.**

## What's working (keep feeding it)

- **The research report is the workhorse.** `/state-of-executive-team-execution/` is our #1 cited page (44 citations) — confirming research is link-bait. *(This is why the 2027 FlagScore primary-data edition matters.)*
- **Our own comparison/"best-of" pages get cited.** `/best-executive-team-development-companies/` (30), `/flag-model-vs-4dx/`, `/flag-model-vs-okrs/`, `/team-execution-frameworks-compared/`. Owning the comparison format works.
- **We own our entity + informational prompts.** "what is the Flag Model" (20% coverage; 41 citations), "leadership team execution framework" (25%), "why isn't my leadership team executing" (40 citations), "what to do when your leadership team isn't executing" (24). On these we're both cited *and* named.
- **Engine-wise, Copilot leads** (3.6% of its citations are us), then ChatGPT (1.6%), Perplexity (1.0%), **Google AI weakest (0.5%)**.

## Recommendations, prioritized

**1. Convert "cited source" → "named provider." (Highest leverage.)** This is the roundup + digital-PR + entity work already kitted — now aim it at the pages AI actually cites:
   - Get Be Legendary *into* the specific cited roundups: **Vistage's "Top Executive Coaching Companies 2026" (66 cites), the Forbes leadership desk, Umbrex, HBS.** (`GEO-ROUNDUP-OUTREACH-KIT.md` + `GEO-DIGITAL-PR-KIT.md`.)
   - Apply the **Wikidata set** (`GEO-WIKIDATA-SET.md`) — making the AI *know* "Be Legendary = executive-team performance firm" is precisely what lifts brand-mention on provider prompts.

**2. Reclaim our own category term: "leadership simulation."** 920 citations, we have 3. Strengthen `/what-is-a-leadership-simulation/` and the Team LFS page (answer-first, more internal links, a citeable definition block), and pitch the term in digital PR. *This ties directly to the LFS product-naming work in flight — the naming decision should keep "leadership simulation" as the discoverable descriptor for exactly this reason.*

**3. Fill the "high-performing executive team" content hole.** Build/optimize a pillar page for "how to build / what makes a high-performing executive team" (788 + 171 citations, zero presence). High-volume, high-intent, buildable now — the clearest content gap in the set.

**4. Keep feeding the citation engine that already works:** expand the comparison/"best-of" family (Track A2) and produce the **2027 research edition with primary FlagScore data** (the research report is our #1 cited asset; primary data is the big re-pitch).

**5. Open two channels AI cites and we ignore:** **LinkedIn** (James's articles/posts + the company page — the #1 source domain) and a few **YouTube** assets (James on the Flag Model / the research findings — the #2 source domain, and we have zero presence).

**6. Engine focus: Google AI (0.5%) and Perplexity (1.0%).** These lean hardest on third-party authority, so they're the clearest read on whether the off-page work (1 & 2) is landing. Copilot at 3.6% shows the ceiling when our pages are trusted.

**7. Measurement cadence.** This report had "no previous period to compare" — the next monthly run will show deltas. Re-pull the Semrush baseline at 90 days (due ~late Jan). Watch **brand coverage** (the 1%) and **Google/Perplexity share** as the KPIs that prove the off-page work is converting citations into recommendations.
