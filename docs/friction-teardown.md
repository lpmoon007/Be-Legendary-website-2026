# Friction & Gaps — Brutal Teardown

A ruthless, buyer's-eye pass (skeptical CEO / EA vetting three firms). Grounded in
the actual site, ordered by conversion cost. Companion to docs/cro-findings.md.

## The friction, worst first
1. **One conversion path, and it's the highest-commitment one.** Every CTA is
   "Book a Calibration Call" → the founder's calendar. No "send me pricing," no
   email capture, no gated download. The strategic brief downloads without asking
   for an email — hand-raisers read the best asset and vanish untracked.
2. **A one-person site selling $75K–$1.1M multi-year engagements never answers
   "who actually delivers this?"** Zero pages introduce anyone but James. The
   scale/continuity/bus-factor objection goes unaddressed on the highest-value offer.
3. **Proof is almost entirely anonymous.** Case studies are "a $350M group," "a
   Fortune 100 team." Only TWO named quotes exist site-wide (Ronn Page, Larry
   Quinlan). Specific numbers (268%, 209%, $1.2B) attached to anonymous companies
   read as possibly-invented to a skeptic.
4. **Vocabulary overload.** The home page alone: Flag Model ×28, plus Calibration
   Call, ELFS, Team LFS, FlagScore, Lost Disciplines, Truth Over Comfort,
   Break-Point, Executive Immersion — ~9 proprietary terms to decode before the
   visitor grasps what they'd buy.
5. **Too many doors, no doorman.** ~10 offers, no simple "which one is right for
   me" chooser. Choice overload stalls the EA who needs one recommendation.
6. **No single pricing page.** Prices are published (rare, good) but scattered
   across coaching-cost, the brief, and how-we-work. The "how much?" buyer hunts.
7. **No concrete "what to expect."** Philosophy is sold; timeline, exec-time,
   deliverables-by-week, "first 30 days" are not. Vagueness = risk on six figures.
8. **The EA (stated #1 buyer) is under-served at the moment of action.** No
   sendable one-pager / "what to tell your principal"; the only action is booking
   the founder's calendar, which she often can't do speculatively.
