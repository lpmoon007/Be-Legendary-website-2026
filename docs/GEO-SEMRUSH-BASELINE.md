**Purpose:** The one-time "where we start" snapshot the GEO Authority Playbook calls for. This is the gap we're closing and the number we'll re-pull in ~90 days to prove movement. The playbook's thesis — *on-page GEO is already excellent; the binding constraint is off-page authority* — is exactly what these numbers confirm.

## What belegendary's backlinks look like today

Top referring domains (by Semrush authority of the linking domain):

`cnn.com` (100) · `bing.com` (91) · `businessinsider.com` (85) · `home.blog` (64) · `summitpost.org` (53) · `cracked.com` (51) · `predictiveindex.com` (50) · `positivityblog.com` (48) · `kbzk.com` (45) · `mariakillam.com` (38)

**Read:** the two genuinely valuable, on-topic links are **cnn.com** and **businessinsider.com** (the press coverage we already cite). The rest are either utility (bing), off-topic (summitpost, cracked, a paint blog), or thin single links. There is **no cluster of leadership/business-authority domains** linking in — which is precisely the cluster roundup inclusion + digital PR + podcast guesting are designed to build.

## Link-gap targets (CCL has these, belegendary doesn't)

High-authority domains linking to CCL but not to belegendary — the realistic subset to chase (the mega-platforms are aspirational, the editorial/reference ones are winnable):

- **Winnable / on-strategy:** `nytimes.com`, `theguardian.com`, `healthline.com`, `indeed.com`, `cambridge.org`, `nih.gov` — earned via digital PR on the research report + expert quotes (Track B1/B3).
- **Platform/profile presence:** `linkedin.com`, `github.com`, `pinterest.com` — mostly profile/directory links (Track B5), easy to establish, low individual value but they corroborate the entity.
- **Aspirational (don't chase directly):** `google.com`, `apple.com`, `microsoft.com`, `adobe.com`, `indiatimes.com` — these come with scale, not outreach; ignore for now.
