# Partner logos

Drop a partner's logo here as `<slug>.svg` (preferred) or `<slug>.png`, then set
`logo: "/partners/<slug>.svg"` on that partner in `challenge/lib/partners.ts`.

Example: for Ledgebrook, add `ledgebrook.svg` here and uncomment the `logo` line
in the `ledgebrook` entry. Until then, the page shows a serif wordmark of the
partner's name.

A transparent-background logo that reads on the dark header works best; it renders
at ~32px tall.
